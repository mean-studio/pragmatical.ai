# 2026 colour research for Pragmatical AI

7 September 2026. Research and proposals only; no application styles changed.

## What the sources support

- Figma's 2026 web-design guide identifies bright, saturated palettes among the current directions. This supports exploring more energetic accents than the muted green prototype. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/web-design-trends/)
- Figma's discussion with Pantone describes its 2026 Cloud Dancer selection as a soft natural white. Neutral foundations are also part of the year's discussion; they can provide space for stronger accents. A colour-of-the-year selection is not a measure of website adoption. [Figma / Pantone](https://www.figma.com/blog/pantone-speaking-the-language-of-color/)
- Adobe's 2026 creative forecast emphasises sensory, emotional, playful and culturally grounded imagery. The implication for this site is to develop distinctive explanatory visuals alongside its palette. [Adobe](https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2025/12/09/four-creative-trends-define-marketing-2026)
- Canva's 2026 forecast favours human expression and deliberate imperfection. For a software company, this can inform an original mark and visual language without compromising precise diagrams or clear interface design. [Canva](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/design-trends-2026/)

These are editorial trend reports, not a ranked census of the most popular technology-brand colours. They support a direction, not claims that a particular hex value is trending or will improve conversions.

## Application principles

Compare the same content, symbol and diagrams in each palette. Colour should not be judged against different compositions. Use the brand accent for emphasis, important actions and selected diagram paths, with neutral space for reading and product evidence. Data-status colours remain a separate semantic system.

Dark mode needs its own calibrated accent and surface contrast. The board's dark backgrounds are comparison samples, not a commitment to another uniformly dark website. Validate text, buttons, focus states and diagrams at their actual sizes before finalising tokens.

The next visual study should join the palette to the real message: lightweight Worker SSR, AI-first interfaces, Studio, automation, topology-led modernisation and distributed deployment. Contract Vetting remains approximately 10–15% of the company narrative. No further UI implementation should proceed on the basis of this palette board alone while the positioning document is under review.
