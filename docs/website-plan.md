# Pragmatical AI website plan

> Superseded for content emphasis and homepage hierarchy by [Company positioning and capabilities](company-positioning-and-capabilities.md). Contract Vetting is now a 10–15% supporting product story; platform, Studio, automation, modernisation and edge delivery form the main narrative.

Planning baseline: 6 September 2026. This document supersedes the product hierarchy in the current redesign prototype. It proposes the corporate website; it does not specify the future Cherga websites or claim that the planned platform consolidation is complete.

## 1. Purpose and positioning

Pragmatical AI is the company creating an AI-first framework, an integrated application platform, and domain products. The corporate website should explain the company's conviction, show tangible work, and direct visitors to the appropriate product destination.

Proposed company statement:

> We build the foundations and tools for AI-first software.

Supporting copy direction:

> From interfaces and agent workflows to understanding existing systems, we bring the capabilities of AI into software people can build, use and trust.

The statement must accommodate new application creation, the UI framework, system understanding and modernisation. Modernisation is a significant capability, not the definition of the company. Services support adoption and partnerships; they do not lead the homepage.

Success: after the first two sections, a visitor can explain what Pragmatical AI builds, identify Cherga as its integrated platform, and choose a relevant next destination. By the following section they understand why Contract Vetting matters.

## 3. Navigation and sitemap

Primary navigation: **Our work · Approach · Company**. Header action: **Get in touch**. Persistent sun/moon icon button with an accessible action label.

Initial public pages:

- `/` — company narrative, two major work features, engineering approach and invitation.
- `/work` — Cherga and Contract Vetting introductions, supporting examples and verified external destinations.
- `/approach` — how we connect interfaces, system context, execution and human review; illustrated with concrete examples.
- `/about` — company purpose, actual people, relevant experience and working principles.
- `/contact` — straightforward route for product enquiries, adoption and partnerships.

Do not launch a journal or news feed with filler. Add a Writing navigation item when substantive engineering articles or company updates exist. Keep useful current service and product routes reachable until their replacements and redirect targets are established.

## 4. Homepage composition

### A. Company opening

One strong headline, a short supporting paragraph and one primary action: **Explore our work**, scrolling to the next section. A quiet text link can lead to the company approach.

Use the proposed company statement as the initial copy candidate, not a final approved slogan. The opening must explicitly communicate building AI-first software. Avoid a hero dominated by migration, an app-builder prompt box, or a generic agent dashboard.

Visual: a restrained, code-native composition linking a small interface, a service and an execution/review sequence. It introduces the relationship between capabilities without becoming a full architecture diagram. Label it as an illustration when it is not a working product capture. It must remain understandable without animation.

### B. Cherga — the primary platform feature

Large feature with the descriptor **AI-first framework. Batteries included.**

Explain the integrated direction in a short paragraph: create interfaces, connect services and agent workflows, and bring existing systems into the same working environment. Name Studio as the workspace. Describe integration as planned or in progress where appropriate.

Give equal conceptual weight to two paths, within this single feature:

- **Create something new:** use the framework directly or work through Studio.
- **Bring what already exists:** discover topology and intent, then plan changes that fit the surrounding system.

Use one substantial visual and concise annotations instead of independent cards for every subsystem. Code Doctor and agent-stack may appear in supporting explanatory copy, not as competing product promotions. Include Cloudflare as the first intended integrated deployment target, below the core promise.

Primary destination: **Explore Cherga**, once the destination is ready. Secondary developer link: **Use the UI framework**, pointing to the verified current or future framework destination with accurate branding.

### C. Contract Vetting — substantial flagship feature

Place directly after Cherga, before principles, other work or company content. Give it comparable visual care and a clear independent identity.

Show a concrete review situation: a returned draft, a changed clause, relevant context/rules, and a reviewer decision. Explain the value of connecting document rounds, evidence, agent outputs and human review through controlled data flow.

Use a real product capture when available. Otherwise, create an explicitly illustrative review sequence. Do not depict a verified legal conclusion, shipping capability or customer result without evidence.

Primary action: **Explore Contract Vetting**, to its dedicated site. Availability must match the product's actual state. Explain that it demonstrates the company's domain engineering; avoid claiming that all future Cherga integration is already implemented.

### D. How we build

Three concise principles, each tied to evidence already shown:

1. **Designed for AI from the start:** a structured framework that supports AI-assisted creation and useful human interfaces.
2. **Context before change:** understand relationships, existing behaviour and constraints before transforming a system.
3. **Connected execution, visible decisions:** connect services, agent work and review with explicit data flow and inspectable outcomes.

Link to `/approach` for detail. Avoid a second product catalogue or a wall of abstract values.

### E. People and company invitation

Short company introduction with verified founder/team experience. Use client logos, quotations or numerical results only with substantiated attribution and appropriate rights; previous employment is not company customer proof.

Close with a concrete invitation to discuss adopting the tools or working with Pragmatical AI. One contact action. Services can be introduced here without changing the primary company story into a consultancy funnel.

## 5. Supporting page briefs

**Our work:** two dominant entries, Cherga and Contract Vetting. Explain the integrated platform direction once. Place GDS Playground beneath the Cherga entry as an example. Give existing experiments and transition-era products a compact secondary area only when they provide useful evidence; do not recreate the old equal-weight product grid.

**Approach:** use two connected examples: creating a new application and evolving an existing system. Explain how framework structure, discovered context and execution support both. Code Doctor's strongest contribution is discovery across the estate and validated intent, not just translation or linting. Explain platform responsibilities without exposing a setup burden to prospective users.

**Company:** purpose, actual people, relevant background and what the company is building toward. No implied frontier-model research programme, invented organisation scale or unsupported claim of uniqueness.

**Contact:** short enquiry form, useful product context and existing verified contact details. Preserve validation and accessible feedback. No mandatory assessment-booking journey.

## 6. Visual direction

- Light-first warm neutral canvas, dark text, restrained indigo accent. Supply an equally intentional dark theme through the existing icon control.
- Retain Figtree as the current working type choice. Use strong scale differences, moderate weights and readable paragraph widths. Validate in the actual composition before choosing another font.
- Spacious editorial layout with one dominant element per section. Cherga and Contract Vetting receive large bespoke compositions; supporting content stays visually quieter.
- Use a subtle woven/interconnected motif sparingly to connect the story. Keep detailed Cherga brand development for its later website plan.
- Use contrast, space and scale to establish focus. Reserve accent-filled buttons for the primary action in each context.
- Prefer product interfaces and meaningful flow illustrations to decorative AI imagery. Any movement should clarify a sequence and respect reduced-motion settings.
- Mobile order: promise, action, illustration, Cherga, Contract Vetting, approach, company. Keep navigation compact and eliminate horizontal overflow.

## 7. Content evidence and transition

Before implementation, inventory the existing product domains, working demos, screenshots, availability labels and company facts. Existing Code Doctor content describes an AWS-first roadmap; the consolidated direction is Cloudflare-first. Reconcile this deliberately rather than copying older claims into the corporate site.

Maintain an internal claim register: current and demonstrated; implemented but not end-to-end verified; planned integration. Translate these distinctions into clear public wording where they affect the visitor's expectations.

Audit old routes before removing or redirecting them. Preserve valuable product explanations during the transition. Redirect only to an appropriate live replacement; don't send every old page to the homepage. Do not rename packages or alter sibling repositories as part of this website task.

## 8. Implementation sequence

1. **Content and route inventory:** verify destinations, availability and usable evidence; map current pages to the proposed structure.
2. **Homepage composition:** implement the company opening, Cherga feature and Contract Vetting feature first. Check focus in desktop and mobile compositions before extending the page.
3. **Complete corporate pages:** write Approach, Our work, Company and Contact around the same hierarchy; revise footer and navigation.
4. **Product evidence and polish:** integrate verified captures, clear illustration labels and restrained interaction.
5. **Validation:** SWC lint/build; responsive inspection; light/dark contrast; keyboard navigation; accessibility; server-rendered content; internal/external destinations; SPA back navigation; metadata and route handling.

Use native SWC declarative components, store bindings and framework layout primitives throughout. Keep theme integration in the existing platform bridge. Do not replace framework composition with imperative DOM rendering or introduce React. Plan changes and website implementation remain separate from publication.

## 9. Acceptance criteria

- Pragmatical AI reads as the company behind the work.
- Cherga communicates the AI-first framework and integrated platform; neither app generation nor modernisation overwhelms its identity.
- Studio, Code Doctor and agent-stack are explained as connected capabilities, with current versus intended integration accurately represented.
- Contract Vetting has a substantial, early, distinct feature and a clear destination.
- Cherga.com and the future ChergaJS destination have clear roles without duplicating their detailed site plans here.
- The page offers obvious visual focus, a usable light/dark icon control and a coherent mobile sequence.
- Claims, links and product availability are verified before publication.
- Existing source and route value is preserved through a deliberate transition.
