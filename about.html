01 — What we build

Applied AI for the systems your business already depends on.

We are not a foundation-model company. We do not train new LLMs. We do not believe the bottleneck in enterprise software is writing more code, more contracts, or more dashboards. We think it is something more specific.

The bottleneck is understanding what you already have. The legacy estate that was never documented. The contract archive that was never reviewed end-to-end. The routes drivers actually take, written down nowhere. The data warehouse whose fields shifted meaning across three migrations.

Across all four product lines, our conviction is the same: AI surfaces patterns at a scale humans cannot. Humans confirm intent in a way AI cannot. The asset — the structured, machine-readable representation of how your business actually works — stays with you.

AI surfaces patterns.
Humans confirm intent.
The asset stays with you.
02 — How we work

Service-led engagements with a platform underneath.

  • 01.
    Discovery first.

    Every engagement begins with an assessment of what you already have. That assessment is the first deliverable, not a pre-engagement marketing exercise. Most organisations have never had a coherent picture of their own estate — and the picture itself is often worth the engagement.

  • 02.
    Customer owns the asset.

    The rulebook is the durable artefact of every engagement. It is machine-readable, exportable, and owned by the customer. The next migration starts from where the last one ended — not from zero. When the engagement ends, the rulebook stays.

  • 03.
    Humans in the loop where it matters.

    For every pattern our platforms surface, a human who still holds the knowledge confirms or corrects it. We do not let LLMs decide intent unsupervised. The platform is built around the reality that institutional knowledge is the source of truth, not against it.

  • 04.
    Honest about scope.

    We do not claim to mind-read codebases, contract archives, route patterns, or warehouses. The intent was never fully in the system to begin with — that is the problem we are built for. Our platforms surface what is in the artefacts; humans confirm what is not. We assume nothing.
